Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who we are

BASED is run by BASED ON VALUES DATING LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales. We are the “controller” of your personal data. Contact: support@baseddatingonline.com.

What we collect

From you: your first name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), date of birth, biological sex, whether you’re married and whether you have children, country and postcode or town (turned into a map position for distances), photos, questionnaire answers, your religion (if you choose to give it), your Search settings, your pre-saved introduction, and the messages and emoji reactions you send.

From your use of BASED: who you show interest in, move on from or block; profiles you view; reports you make; when you last read each chat; when you last had BASED open (so we can see how many members are online); your plan and its status; and your consents.

From others: messages other members send you, reports made about you, your payment status from Stripe, and your age and identity check results from Didit.

Why we use it

To run BASED for you (our contract with you, the Terms and Conditions): showing your profile, working out compatibility, letting matches message each other, managing your plan and sending service emails.

Your religion, photos and messages are “special category data”, which we use only with your explicit consent, given when you joined.

To keep members safe (our legitimate interest): age and identity checks, investigating reports and misuse, and limits that stop abuse.

To send you updates and offers, only if you opted in. And to meet our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Other members see your profile, as shown in the app. Your exact location, postcode and email are never shown to them. We use these providers, who handle data for us under contract: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting the app), Stripe (payments), Didit (age and identity checks), Resend (emails), and Postcodes.io and Photon (turning a postcode or town into a map position). We never sell your data and we don’t run adverts.

Automated decisions

Compatibility scores decide the order profiles appear in, and your filters decide which profiles you see. We don’t make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means alone.

Before BASED opens near you

BASED opens area by area. If you sign up before your area opens, we keep your profile, answers and photos so they’re ready on opening day, but nobody can see them until then. We’ll email you when your area opens. You can change or delete your profile at any time while you wait.

How long we keep it

While you have an account. When you delete it, everything is deleted straight away, except: conversations under an open safety report; if a report about you is open, a private copy of your account until 90 days after the report is closed; a one-way fingerprint of your email with your free-allowance usage, so allowances don’t reset if you re-join; and payment records Stripe must keep by law.

Your rights

You can find out how we use your data, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, pause (restrict) its use, object to how we use it, take it to another service, and withdraw your consent at any time. Use the options on the Support page, or email support@baseddatingonline.com. We reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
